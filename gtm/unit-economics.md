## Costs to budget *outside* the per-bag margin

- **Slotting / promotional allowances** (grocery more than club).
- **Road Show / demo cost** per warehouse (staff, samples, product) — treat as customer-acquisition cost.
- **Spoilage / returns reserve** (frozen has waste; hold ~2–5%).
- **Insurance** ($2–5M product/general liability — annual premium).
- **Certifications** (GFSI/SQF audit costs — usually the co-packer's, confirm).
- **Working capital** — club pays on terms while you pay the co-packer up front; a won order needs cash to fund the production run. This is the silent killer; model it.

## Sensitivity questions to answer

1. At what **production run size** does COGS drop enough to clear 30%? (Volume is your margin lever.)
2. What's the **all-in demo cost per warehouse**, and how many bags must sell to pay it back?
3. If a warehouse moves **X units/week**, can the co-packer supply it with your lead time — and can you **finance** that run?
4. Does a **dip-included** or **larger multipack** SKU improve value perception enough to raise price above $11.99?

## Pack-size decision

Your stated retail pack is **10–20 count**. For **club**, bias to the **high end or a multipack** (e.g., ~24–30 ct / ~2–2.5 lb, or two bags) so the shelf price delivers obvious club value. Keep the **10–20 ct** pack for regular grocery and independents.
