Go-to-market strategy for selling frozen coxinhas into **club stores**, starting in **Utah** and expanding region by region.

## The one-paragraph version
Position the product as a **universal air-fryer chicken snack** (not an ethnic specialty — Utah's Brazilian demand is thin). Clear the two hard gates first: **USDA-inspected production** (coxinhas are a poultry product) and **unit economics that survive Costco's ~14% markup cap** at a **~$14.99 premium 20–24 count bag** (~$0.65/piece — priced to real value, not the bottom of the club band). Then use **Costco Road Shows** and local demos as the wedge to **prove velocity**, convert that data into a **Mountain-region buyer** slot, and **expand region by region**.

## The three things that gate everything
1. **USDA co-packer** — no legal production without it. Solve first.
2. **Margin at $14.99 (20–24 ct)** — club is dead if COGS won't clear ~30% after freight. Solve second.
3. **Supply + working capital** — a won order you can't fill or finance burns the buyer. Solve before pitching.
